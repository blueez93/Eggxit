## 1.0.0 — First Hatch

- Explore caves and other underground habitats to discover rare 3D eggs.
- Bring your eggs home and learn what helps each family hatch.
- Meet the Baby Spider, Spider Mount, Baby Bat and Baby Warden. Fill your collection with their available variants.
- Keep a personal illustrated bestiary with habitat notes, portraits and collection counters.
- Play in Minecraft Java 1.21.1 on NeoForge with GeckoLib 4.8, in single-player or on a dedicated server.
